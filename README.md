# Summon Raging Spirit

A campaign and atlas companion for a Necromancer in Path of Exile 3.30, Covens of Umbra.

The guide is at <https://pharanyx-labs.github.io/poe-summon-raging-spirit/>.

You start as a Witch. Rolling Magma is the button until the Mud Flats. Summon Raging Spirit is the button from there through maps. The endgame shape follows the public Allflame profile [Shaemora](https://poe.ninja/poe1/profile/packetsynclabs-1644/allflame/character/Shaemora).

Press n for the next step, p for the previous step, and d to mark the step done. Checkmarks stay in your browser.

Passive names are from the 3.29 tree. The atlas steps follow the 3.30 rules and do not invent wheel names.
